# JezLauncher privacy policy

JezLauncher does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries, and no accounts.

## Data on your device

Your notes and routines, home screen and widget layout, hidden and renamed apps and all settings are stored only in the app's storage on your phone. Screen time (if you grant usage access) is read from Android and shown on the home screen; it is not stored elsewhere or sent anywhere.

If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in that backup. This is done by Android and your backup provider, not by JezLauncher.

## Network

JezLauncher uses the network only for the daily wallpaper, and only while **Daily new wallpaper** is turned on:

- **Default, NASA APOD:** at most one request a day to `science.nasa.gov` for the latest Astronomy Picture of the Day posts, then the image download from NASA's servers.
- **Your own feed:** if you set a Wallpaper URL, the app downloads that JSON and the images it lists, from the servers you chose.

These requests contain nothing about you or your phone beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address); the servers' own privacy policies apply to them. Nothing else in the app connects to the internet.

Links you open from the app (for example the project page or a web search from the app drawer) open in your browser.

## Permissions

- **Accessibility service** (optional): used only to lock the screen with a double tap. It does not read, store or send screen content.
- **Usage access** (optional): only to show today's screen time on the home screen.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, full screen alerts:** only for the reminders you set on notes.
- Widgets on the widget wall are run by the apps that provide them; their own privacy policies apply.
