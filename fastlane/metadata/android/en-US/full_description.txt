<p>JezLauncher is a minimalist, ad-free Android launcher with a few practical extras: a wall for widgets, notes that can remind you, and an optional daily wallpaper.</p>

<p>It is an independent fork of the open source Olauncher by tanujnotes and keeps its text-only home screen.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Minimal home screen:</b> a short list of apps, app drawer with search, hidden apps, renaming, work profile and Private Space support, light and dark themes.</li>
<li><b>Widget wall:</b> swipe right for a 4-column grid of widgets. Long press to edit, drag to move, drag the edges to resize. Works with regular Android widgets from other apps.</li>
<li><b>Notes:</b> swipe left to jot something down, strike it through when done, drag to reorder.</li>
<li><b>Reminders:</b> give a note a time and make it one-off, daily or weekly. Choose how it alerts: a notification, a full screen alert over the lock screen, or an alarm that rings until you mark it done, snooze or dismiss it.</li>
<li><b>Routines:</b> daily and weekly notes reset when the next period starts, and notes still to do can show on the home screen.</li>
<li><b>Wallpaper:</b> optional daily wallpaper from NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day, or from your own JSON feed URL.</li>
<li><b>Readable text:</b> the home screen text colour is taken from the wallpaper and kept readable on it.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts. Notes and settings stay on your device. The network is used only to download the daily wallpaper, and only if you turn it on.</p>

<p><b>Permissions</b></p>
<p>The launcher lists your installed apps, can ask Android to uninstall an app, and can open the notification shade. Reminders use notifications, exact alarms and full screen alerts. Usage access (screen time on the home screen), the accessibility service and device administrator (both only for double tap to lock) are optional and off until you enable them.</p>
